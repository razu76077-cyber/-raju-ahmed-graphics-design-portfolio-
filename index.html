Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about working together? Find answers below.

What is your typical project delivery time?
Project turnaround time depends on the scope. A standard logo design usually takes 2 to 3 days, while complete brand identity systems and packaging projects take between 5 to 7 days. Rush delivery options are also available upon request.
What is your revision policy?
I offer unlimited revisions during the initial design phase until you are 100% satisfied with the concept. My goal is to ensure the final output matches your exact vision before finalizing source files.
What file formats do you provide upon completion?
You will receive all standard industry-standard production-ready files including AI, PSD, EPS, SVG, PDF, high-res PNG (transparent background), and JPG.
How does the payment process work?
For direct contracts, payments are typically split into a 50% upfront deposit and 50% upon final project approval. For platform-based orders (Fiverr/Upwork), escrow secure payments are used.
